## sensory

Here we go. The smell.

You live in a neighborhood where the streetlights were installed over a century ago in the old cotton fields. A little dusty motor in a metal case with a handle stands behind a locked door. You walk past the electric line. Suddenly the light goes green and you hear a knock at the door, and the door closes behind you. The streetlights go on, the traffic lights go on, you step on the cord, and then it dies. The whole neighborhood panics, and suddenly there is an explosion.

You describe it as a brilliant blink followed by a whistling sound. You never see a person. Your house is dark in that same moment because you never saw your parents. The streetlights go off again, the traffic lights stay green, and your breath comes back to you, and your body stops sweating and goes dark for a moment.

The reason: that event was never recorded, was never even filmed, and you are the only witness who saw it happen, not a clerk who recorded it. The fact that it happened is evidence that it happened, not a statement about the event. The evidence is the sound, the light, the heat, and the strange, whistling-like sound. That's the whole story.
