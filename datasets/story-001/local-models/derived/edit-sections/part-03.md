The air in the service corridor tasted like old oil and wet dust, thick enough to coat the back of the throat. Flickering lights cast long, jerky shadows against the corrugated walls, making the space feel deeper than it was. I stood before the maintenance hatch, a small, rectangular mouth in the industrial skin of the factory. My hand hovered over the metal plate, a habit from the childhood city-zone boundary, a rhythm I'd learned to keep the lights on when the grid failed. I counted three, tapped twice, then held my breath. The metal didn't groan; it simply yielded, sliding open with a sound like a bone cracking. Inside, the dead-looking service corridor stretched out, a tunnel of grey concrete and rusted pipes. It was silent, save for the low, dying hum of a machine that shouldn't have been running. As I stepped through, my boot caught on a loose plate near the threshold. I twisted my ankle, a sharp spike of pain shooting up my leg. I stumbled forward, losing my balance. In the reflex of falling, my elbow slammed into a control panel I hadn't noticed before. A small, unmarked lever. Nothing happened at first. Then, a sudden jolt of electricity ran through my body, not painful, but jarring, like static from a storm. The hum in the corridor didn't just grow louder; it sharpened, rising into a high-pitched whine that made my teeth ache. The lights didn't flicker on; they snapped to a brilliant, blinding green, then died instantly. The silence was shattered by that brilliant blink, followed immediately by the whistling sound. It wasn't a machine sound, not really. It was the sound of something old waking up, or maybe something trying to scream. The whole corridor went chaotic in a fraction of a second, the air pressure dropping so hard my ears popped. I couldn't see my companion anywhere, just the swirling dust and the heat rising from the floor. The hum grew even louder, a ticking time bomb counting down. My companion was counting on me. I took a deep breath and stepped forward, ready to face whatever danger lay ahead. The silence was shattered by a brilliant blink, followed by a whistling sound. My companion was in danger, and I couldn't let this disaster claim them.
