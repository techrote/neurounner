### 4. Causality Chain
*Logical flow of events, acknowledging potential hallucinations in the chain.*

1.  **Trigger:** Brownout approaches; Sprocket smells insulation.
2.  **Inciting Incident:** Green light blink + door close. Sprocket crosses boundary.
3.  **Catalyst:** Improvised entry into maintenance hatch using knock-code.
4.  **Conflict:** Conveyor reactivates due to tiny error; begins misclassifying items (dangerous cycle).
5.  **Complication:** Friend trapped on wrong side of line; timing noise prevents safe passage.
6.  **Action:** Sprocket intervenes using salvaged conductor + childhood trick to hijack control signal.
7.  **Climax:** Line cascades. Sprocket must choose: save friend or stop line.
8.  **Resolution:** Line stops (friend may die or survive); Sprocket suffers physical cost.
9.  **Aftermath:** Memory returns with one missing noise.

### 6. Voice Constraints
*All text must adhere to these rules. No generic cyberpunk tropes.*

*   **Syntax:** Fragmented, sensory-first. Start sentences with nouns or smells. Avoid "I felt" or "I saw." Use "The smell. The sound."
*   **Tone:** Unfiltered, raw, slightly detached. Like a log entry that isn't a log.
*   **Vocabulary:** Industrial, tactile. Words like *insulation, conductor, knock-code, sorting, cascade, whistling, green light.* Avoid *hologram, neon, matrix, data stream.*
*   **Perspective:** Strictly first-person. The narrator is the only witness. "The fact that it happened is evidence that it happened."
*   **Uncertainty Marker:** Use `[?]` or ellipses `...` to indicate missing links. Do not resolve the ambiguity.
*   **Constraint:** Name the thing first, then the memory. If the name is missing, leave it missing.

### 7. Final Drafting Brief

**Objective:** Draft a 3,000-word fictional recollection based on the provisional board.

**Execution Steps:**
1.  **Opening:** Start with the smell of warm insulation. Establish the present trigger immediately. Do not explain the past yet.
2.  **The Journey:** Move through the geography (City-Zone -> Boundary -> Autofactory). Use the "long-winding" vs. "shortcut" confusion to create spatial disorientation.
3.  **The Incident:** Describe the reactivation of the conveyor. Focus on the *mechanical* nature of the danger. The line is making a "wrong turn."
4.  **The Rescue:** Introduce the friend. Keep their identity vague. Describe the "noisy timing problem."
5.  **The Fix:** Detail the use of the salvaged conductor and the "childhood trick." This is the moment of imperfect competence.
6.  **The Choice:** The tradeoff. Save the friend or stop the line. Show the physical consequence (burn, limp, pain).
7.  **The Ending:** The factory movement ceases. End with the memory gap—the missing noise.
8.  **Review:** Check against the Uncertainty Register. Ensure no generic cyberpunk filler exists. Ensure the "evidence" (sound, light, heat) is the only thing that feels real.

**Forbidden:**
*   Do not invent the friend's name.
*   Do not clarify the "childhood trick."
*   Do not resolve the "missing noise."
*   Do not use terms like "cyberpunk" or "futuristic."
