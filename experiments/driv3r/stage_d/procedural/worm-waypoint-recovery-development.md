## Frozen protocol and first-class artifacts

- Input states: six unchanged geometry families (`open`, `slats`, `ring`, `canals`, `baffled`, `crucible`) × seeds 23/79 × source-replayed tick 60, with a frozen, legally reachable geodesic waypoint and deterministic noise. Same 32 decisions, hard permissions, scalar guide, start ink, pen cadence and candidate cardinal actions in all arms.
- Arms were defined before viewing outcomes: no-guide sham; previous twelve-step +8192 reference; full 32-step +8192; full 32-step +16384; bounded hysteretic rescue (+4096 with up to twelve boosted +24576 decisions on four non-improving cursor moves); and a clearly distinguished deterministic geodesic-action **oracle control**. The oracle does not simulate a model.
- One chosen candidate per step; reversible cursor movement is not a material Undo. Events retain source-target geodesic candidate, every legal offer, guidance delta, chosen movement, logical visits, pen state and before/after synthetic material hashes. Explicit `UNDO_MATERIAL_EDIT_UNAVAILABLE` event has no native receipt. Original synthetic PNGs and case JSON/manifest are rederived byte-for-byte, including on tamper-and-rehash attacks.
- All outcomes, including the unchanged sham and one hysteresis failure, stay in the denominator. Goals are hit if visited at any step; *goal arrival does not stop further drawing*. This matters for post-arrival oscillations.

**Principal negative finding:** At +16384 for the full horizon, the nominally soft guide produces **exactly the same 32-step trajectory and synthetic material as the hard geodesic oracle on all twelve states**. This controller is effectively a deterministic shortest-path follower on this small pilot, not evidence of a satisfyingly deranged wormbrain. Even +8192 throughout reaches 12/12 with substantially altered post-arrival paths, whereas 12 early nudges reach only 1/12. The intervention schedule, not simply guide availability, determines apparent goal success.

**Separate tradeoff:** Hysteresis reaches 11/12, but it applies nonzero guidance at all 384 candidate-choice opportunities and uses 138 high-gain decisions. It does not yet demonstrate lower decision cost or superior style. The deterministic oracle reaches within 7–20 moves, and therefore spends the remaining forced 32-step budget oscillating around the waypoint; this can produce many repeats. Do not mislabel oscillatory ink as achieved quality or count the oracle as a fair autonomous artistic controller.

Next experiments should score changed composition and post-goal continuation, not just waypoint arrival.
