# D-2 — assessor-blind visual-preference pilot (DEVELOPMENT only)

**Purpose.** Prior D-2 pulse experiments establish causal synthetic controller sensitivity but not whether changed lines are interesting. Stop tuning the controller on area/turn metrics alone. Prepare a small, source-replayable, neutral presentation for later human assessment. This is not a learned-model, native material or artistic-acceptance result.

**Input custody.** This study consumes original input-frozen six 80×80 scene families (`open`, `slats`, `ring`, `canals`, `baffled`, `crucible`) × seeds 23 and 79. Its parent 32-decision pulse experiment is `07399c60873e35a7e12e4660bc63a926c9f5d701`, with exact dependency Git blob custody in the study's JSON. `worm_blind_gallery_study.py` recomputes the original baseline/material hashes before any fork. No shared contract, native sources, other lanes or frozen original evidence are mutated.

**Sampling fixed before judging.** For each of 12 scene/seed pairs, use tick 12 for seed 23 and tick 108 for seed 79; three paired arms: zero pulse (identical-image sham), +4,096 u16, +8,192 u16. The pulse lasts one decision; the remainder of 32 cardinal decisions uses unmodified guidance. Exactly **36 review items** with 12 unmarked sham catch trials are emitted regardless of whether interventions visually change the result. Item order is deterministic SHA-256 pseudorandom ordering of predeclared cases. Left/right baseline placement is balanced 18/18 and concealed from reviewers; this is *assessor-blinding*, not secure cryptographic secrecy. At most two human review dimensions are needed: **pairwise preference for weird/interesting line composition** (`left/tie/right`) and **visible difference** (`none/small/clear`). A third optional line-weirdness vote may be used; never infer steering controllability from still images alone.

**Output separation.** Give assessors only `review/`, containing `item-NNN-context.png`, `item-NNN-left.png`, `item-NNN-right.png`, and an unfilled anonymous `ballot.json`. Do **not** provide `custody/assignments.json` until ballots are locked: it contains actual policy labels, input origins, source-preregistration SHA, original material SHA, original guidance pulse strength, matched generated geometry and every image hash. The two sides are rendered with the same pixel palette/scale and starting context. The reviewer packet deliberately contains no active-arm/seed/scene/tick labels. Read-only reviewers should not inspect generator source before scoring.

**Analysis plan, not yet executed.** Retain all sham and unchanged interventions. Report all nonzero arms' preference counts, ties, visual-difference counts, sham false-difference rate and per-family disagreements. Treat a sham's asserted visible difference as an assessor/presentation-quality failure; do not drop it silently. Pairwise preference is subjective, and the review sample's deterministic seeds and scenes are DEVELOPMENT, not a sealed held-out evaluation. No artistry scores exist yet, no claim of statistical independence across related sketches or model benefit.

**Safety/qualification.** `python -m unittest -v test_worm_blind_gallery_study` must pass. `python worm_blind_gallery_study.py --out EMPTY_DIR` produces source-replayable image bytes; `verify()` regenerates every item and rejects changed images even if the attacker edits the custody-file hashes. Shams must be byte-identical. The output includes no native material edits, actual UNDO receipts, model calls, training, GPU/API work or Actions dispatch. Keep the disabled 2/4/8-runner matrix inert. Remain Draft/HOLD.
