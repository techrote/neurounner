## Loader discipline and future work

Trainers may use **only TRAIN decision observation fields** to compose inputs and their `target_action` for supervision. They must **not** concatenate whole JSONL rows to the prompt: `candidate_scores`, `receipt`, `target_action`, world metadata and hard-negative solution tags are targets/analysis, not observable policy inputs. DEV is evaluation only, the new synthetic heldout is embargoed, and W0 imported references are independent evaluation only. Model/checkpoint pins and original source hashes belong in a separate training receipt.

Recommended next measurement: compare a bounded *existing primary-owner* optimizer run against no-training control and matched-seed legal/random baselines; report distinct action diversity, goal, recovery and motif PNGs. No new optimizer was launched by this factory. A hosted Actions CI gate, corpus artifact upload to GitHub Actions, third-party review, and read-only native source-backed sample translation remain open.
