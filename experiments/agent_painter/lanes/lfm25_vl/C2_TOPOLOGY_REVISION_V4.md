## Why a different topology, not further DEVELOPMENT-coordinate fitting

The independent [reviewer C2c P2](https://github.com/techrote/cybersand/pull/575#issuecomment-6886936339) showed that C2b and C2c have the **same entire graph signature**. The corrected C2c run proved source/compiler qualification, not independent group identity. Preserve all C2/C2b/C2c source, failed CI, original failed native producer, and their receipts unchanged. **C2c is a spatial variant of C2b's single group**, never a separate training group. Its centreline proximity to one DEVELOPMENT manifold is not an objective for iterative numeric optimization.

C2d deliberately changes *adjacency*, before evaluating native comparisons. It depicts a branching hanging assembly rather than a long horizontal manifold or another two-cross/offset-Z graph:

- One dominant vertical mast at x48 with a **single genuine degree-four cross** at (48,42), its own top/bottom terminals, and left/right service arms.
- The left branch reaches a **degree-three tee** at (19,42), dividing into a left terminal and a long downward leg.
- The right branch reaches a right-angle **degree-two elbow** at (76,42), drops to a second **degree-three tee** at (76,70) with two horizontal terminal arms.
- Graph degrees **[1×6, 2×1, 3×2, 4×1]**, exactly 10 nodes / nine edges, six endpoints, one connected component, zero cycles, nominal three-cell diameter, localized fittings. The earlier C2b/C2c graph is **[1×6, 2×2, 4×2]**, whereas original held-out three-riser has three degree-three tees and five leaves.
- All nine edges are orthogonal and at least 15 cells long. The deliberate negative-space bays between hanging branches and the upright mast must survive 3-cell tube expansion and seeded fittings. The host compiler and original native Art must verify that; source design alone cannot.

These graph differences are necessary but **not sufficient** for global structural uniqueness. In particular, a central cross+two tees may share a *motif* with some historical Pipework even when the coarse signature differs.
