## Method

Eight independently reinitialized and trained real `4→24→24→1` neural donors across **two new source seeds `29002117`, `29002119`**, two *existing* wet costs 0.35/2.30 and two replicas/source/law. Each donor is supervised on 8,192 new synthetic tile samples (wet flag, fee, nuisance textures; true material costs as **training targets only**) for 1,000 AdamW steps at LR .002/batch 256. Donor training MSE spans **0.0000523–0.0007582**. Never uses previous acceptance observations.

At inference the new donors' **learned** material estimates are extracted using the original deterministic 5×5 Gauss–Legendre texture quadrature at donor-only fee 0.15; real observed fees and wet masks are restored. Three original H19 trained receivers are completely frozen. A world pair changes **only** the law-conditioned learned donor signal; the geometry, goal, legal actions, textures, fees and receiver weights remain identical. No law labels, family IDs, oracle costs/actions, source IDs or answer tokens are given to the receiver.
