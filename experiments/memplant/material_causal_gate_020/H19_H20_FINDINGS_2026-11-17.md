## Mechanism

Fixed **5×5 Gauss–Legendre integration** over the two *texture-nuisance* inputs (their supported [-1,1] domain) interrogates **genuine frozen neural donors** at reference fee 0.15 and each dry/wet material input. Fuse the two independently trained same-law donors, then restore the *actual observed* fees and wet masks. H19 retrained three genuine unchanged-size **209-parameter receiver** networks (26011639/40/41), using the original H16 training worlds with both divergent and invariant counterfactual pairs and two genuine donor training groups; the third donor group was withheld. H20 froze those exact networks and donor extraction and used distinct untouched fee-distribution families. No law labels, family/source IDs, oracle costs or action answers enter inference. Within each counterfactual the geometry, fees, textures, goal, legal masks and receiver weights are identical: **only donor law differs**. This is a strong task-specific *two-material cost-law inductive bias*, **not** general learned-memory transplantation.

## Qualification, evidence and limitations

Independent, non-mutating receipt auditors recalculated oracle costs, observation hashes, all controls and the 30-cell verdicts from **all 9,600 distinct H19/H20 world pairs**, with **38/38** H19 and **31/31** H20 original manifest entries verified. An independently implemented frozen-checkpoint replay reloaded all genuine donors and the three trained receivers, recomputed quadrature estimates, and reproduced **19,200 primary/foreign neural decisions, 9,600 shuffled decisions and 9,600 zero/blind decisions per cohort** with zero logit/action difference. H20 was confirmed disjoint from all 4,800 H19 acceptances, and locally available H11–H18 and invalidated H13 observations were excluded.

**Causal-interpretation boundary:** This is a switch between *separately trained* genuine donor checkpoints, not a surgical change to one scalar within an otherwise identical donor network. Withheld same-law donor-source shams reduce—but cannot eliminate—donor-training confounds. The engineered fixed material probe already yields perfect learned-donor cost-comparator decisions (4,800/4,800 on each cohort), so most of the improvement is in the donor rule extractor, not a general policy learned by the receiver.

**Qualification limitations:** complete H8–H10 historical receipts were unavailable for exhaustive overlap exclusion; original producer source/checkpoints/raw receipts and independent auditors are retained in the owner handoff archive, **not** as a GitHub Actions artifact. No pinned exact-HEAD PyTorch-2.5.1 Actions reproduction or external methodological review yet. H20 uses fresh fee profiles but the *same* three receiver/donor seed families; it does not establish donor-train-seed generalization. The task-specific material probe is a deliberately explicit inductive bias. This is not physical game-law discovery, generic model-state transfer, multi-step navigation or CyberSand integration.
