## Independent audit and mechanism attribution

- Receipt-only independent audit verified **all 4,800 raw counterfactual records**, all 30 independent gate decisions and the original experiment manifest's **31/31 files**.
- Independently computed frozen neural checkpoint replay matched **9,600 primary + 9,600 withheld same-law source-group neural decisions**, donor-conditioned forecasts, shuffled donor actions, zero-conditioned and source-blind actions; **0 action disagreements**, maximum absolute logit difference **0** locally.
- H17's seed-39 wet-rich invariant failures: **24** incorrectly ranked expensive-law actions. For **15/24**, the authentic neural donor-cost comparator ranked the correct route but the trained receiver ranked the wrong one; the other **9/24** were also incorrect at the donor-cost comparator. The training-receiver interaction, not merely raw donor-law estimator bias, limits this confirmation.

The two zero/source-blind controls are *logically weak on opposite-oracle divergent worlds*, since a fixed action cannot be simultaneously oracle-correct for both laws. They are not independent evidence of source use; genuine pre-inference donor shuffling and withheld same-law neural donor-group controls are more informative.
