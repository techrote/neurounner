## Next scientific gates

1. **Source-delta portability:** investigate why an edit accurate on the original scenarios lost much of its selectivity on eight new source-world families; compare contextual addressable K/V structure and stronger controls.
2. **Recipient causal inference:** DG003 does not show reliable unseen joint consequences. Test a different capable receiver or a better-instrumented reasoning oracle rather than optimising against the previously inspected six families.
3. **Real heterogeneous native KV:** expand same-Qwen cache custody into independently trained donor/receiver translation with source-sensitive controls, exact position/mask semantics, and end-to-end cost reporting.
4. **Creative utility:** the Garden functional improvement belongs to the deterministic symbolic compiler; test richer world models and actual inventions that benefit from model-supplied structure rather than only rule-to-field repair.

**Bottom line:** numerical source-specific conditioning and partial feature-space surgery are demonstrated in small synthetic settings. Heterogeneous native cache transplant, general compositional rule reasoning, live latent surgery and generational inheritance are not.
