# See What I See, Know What I Think: Dense Latent Communication Across Heterogeneous Agents

**Primary reference:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13594

## Brief summary report
Examines KV-cache communication between heterogeneous agents when they share observations and when only a donor has seen certain evidence; includes three-agent document sharing.

## MEMPLANT relevance
A key test fixture for MEMPLANT's multi-agent memory-shard and unseen-evidence graft goals.

## Scope and limitations
Not proof that independently produced caches can be joined by concatenating their tensors.

## Recommended controlled experiment
Compare shared-observation and donor-only-observation transplant, with rare-fact QA.
